\# ADR-0006 — Camera Architecture



Status: Accepted



Date: 2026-07-16



\---



\# Context



Project KATANA requires a third-person camera that is responsive, predictable, and gameplay-focused.



The camera must remain independent from movement, combat, animation, and UI systems while supporting future extensions such as lock-on, camera shake, and cinematic sequences.



The project architecture emphasizes clear subsystem boundaries and engine abstraction.



\---



\# Decision



The camera system will be implemented as a dedicated CameraController.



CameraController owns:



\- Camera positioning

\- Camera rotation

\- Camera collision

\- Camera follow behavior



CameraController does NOT own:



\- Player input

\- Character management

\- Combat logic

\- Animation logic



Raw mouse input is owned exclusively by InputController.



Character references are provided exclusively through CharacterController.



Camera tuning values are stored inside CameraConfig.



The camera operates using Roblox's Scriptable camera mode and updates during RenderStepped.



Camera collision is resolved through raycasting.



\---



\# Consequences



Benefits:



\- Clear subsystem responsibilities.

\- High maintainability.

\- Easy future expansion.

\- Gameplay tuning separated from implementation.

\- Reduced coupling to Roblox engine APIs.



Trade-offs:



\- Slightly more modules than a monolithic implementation.

\- Small amount of coordination required between controllers.



\---



\# Future Work



Future versions may introduce:



\- Lock-on camera

\- Camera shake

\- Sprint camera effects

\- Dynamic shoulder switching

\- Cinematic cameras

\- Camera state machine

\- Camera transitions
