\# ADR-0008 — Gameplay Driven Animation



Status: Accepted



Date: 2026-07-16



\---



\# Context



Gameplay systems require consistent visual feedback without allowing animation to influence gameplay logic.



\---



\# Decision



AnimationController will consume StateMachine.



Gameplay determines state.



Animation visualizes state.



Animation must never become the source of truth.



\---



\# Consequences



Benefits:



\- Predictable behavior.

\- Easy debugging.

\- Animation independence.

\- Future network friendliness.



Trade-offs:



\- Gameplay and animation remain separate systems.
